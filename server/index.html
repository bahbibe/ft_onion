The Onion Road

Long before packets, caravans carried silk, glass and gunpowder across Asia in secret, each merchant only ever knowing the trader on either side of him — never the whole route.

This page travels the same way: wrapped in layer after layer of encryption, relayed hand to hand through the Tor network, so that no single point along the road ever sees where it began or where it ends.
